Group of low mountains rising above 1,200 m, standing 10 mi E of the Allegheny Mountains in the Ford Ranges, Marie Byrd Land. Discovered and photographed on aerial flights in 1940 by the USAS and named for Clark University, Worcester, MA (https://data.aad.gov.au/aadc/gaz/scar/display_name.cfm?gaz_id=123589).
