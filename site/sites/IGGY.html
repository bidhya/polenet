A range of mountains extending S from Nimrod Glacier for 50 mi along the western edge of Marsh Glacier. Named for J.H. "Bob" (now Sir J. Holmes) Miller, a member of the N.Z. party of the CTAE (1958) who, with G.W. Marsh, mapped this area (https://data.aad.gov.au/aadc/gaz/scar/display_name.cfm?gaz_id=128909).
