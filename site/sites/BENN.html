Two rock nunataks 0.5 mi apart, lying 0.5 mi N of Lackey Ridge in the Ohio Range, Horlick Mountains. Surveyed by the USARP Horlick Mountains Traverse party in Dec. 1958. Named by US-ACAN for John B. Bennett, geomagnetist-seismologist at Byrd Station, 1960 (https://data.aad.gov.au/aadc/gaz/scar/display_name.cfm?gaz_id=122409).
