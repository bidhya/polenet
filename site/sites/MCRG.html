Miller Crag

A bold and conspicuous outcropping of bare rock (1,450 m), standing 3 mi WSW of Sutley Peak in the W extremity of the Jones Mountains. Mapped by the University of Minnesota-Jones Mountains Party, 1960-61, who named it for Thomas P. Miller, geologist with the party. (https://data.aad.gov.au/aadc/gaz/scar/display_name.cfm?gaz_id=128899).
