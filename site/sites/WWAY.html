Closest operations HUB: 967km to ALE Union Glacier Camp

Group of rocky nunataks extending for 7 mi and marking the S side of the mouth of Recovery Glacier. First seen from the air and visited in 1957 by the CTAE and so named because it was uncertain which route from the nunataks would lead furthest inland (https://data.aad.gov.au/aadc/gaz/scar/display_name.cfm?gaz_id=133622).
