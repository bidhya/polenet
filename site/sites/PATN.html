Former Projects: JPL: Marie Byrd Land

Pyramidal mountain about 2 mi NE of Mount Schlossbach, at the NE end of the S group of the Rockefeller Mountains on Edward VII Peninsula. Discovered by the ByrdAE (1928-30) and later named by Byrd for Seward M. Paterson, manufacturer who furnished shoes and ski boots for the ByrdAE (1933-35) (https://data.aad.gov.au/aadc/gaz/scar/display_name.cfm?gaz_id=129898).
