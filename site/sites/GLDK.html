Gould Knoll

Station IDGLDK
CoordinatesS 72.233, W 100.588

Closest operations HUB: 844km to WAIS

A mostly ice-covered rock knoll that rises on the E margin of Hale Glacier, Thurston Island, at the point the glacier enters Abbot Ice Shelf. Named by US-ACAN after William G. Gould, NOAA specialist, 1960s to the mid 1990s, in the archiving of Advanced Very High Resolution Radiometer satellite images of the world, including those used for AVHRR image maps of the Antarctic continent. (https://data.aad.gov.au/aadc/gaz/scar/display_name.cfm?gaz_id=136060).
