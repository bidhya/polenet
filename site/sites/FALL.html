A chain of nunataks 10 mi long, located 10 mi NE of Harold Byrd Mountains, between the edge of Ross Ice Shelf and Watson Escarpment. Named by US-ACAN for Lt. (jg) Paul R. Fallone, Jr., USN, aide to the Commander, U.S. Naval Support Force, Antarctica, 1962 (https://data.aad.gov.au/aadc/gaz/scar/display_name.cfm?gaz_id=124977).
