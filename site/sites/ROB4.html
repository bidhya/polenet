Coordinate: S 77.0344, E 163.1901

Closest operations HUB: 124km to McMurdo Station

Cape at the S side of the entrance to Granite Harbor on the coast of Victoria Land. Discovered by the South Magnetic Pole Party, led by David, of the BrAE (1907-09) and named for William C. Roberts, assistant zoologist and cook for the expedition (https://data.aad.gov.au/aadc/gaz/scar/display_name.cfm?gaz_id=130851).
