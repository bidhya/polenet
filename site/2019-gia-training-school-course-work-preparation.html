General Training School Preparation

Participants will utilize open-source GIA modeling software SELEN (a SealEveL EquatioN solver) during the training school.  The most recent version, SELEN4, is now available in beta form.  Participants should download and begin working with SELEN4 prior to the school.

The code and user guide are available here:   https://zenodo.org/record/3339209 Additionally, please see the recently submitted SELEN4 companion paper and supplemental material: Spada and Melini, 2019 (submitted) Spada and Melini, 2019 (submitted) - Supplementary Material For questions about working with SELEN4, please contact Giorgio Spada (giorgio.spada@gmail.com) or Daniele Melini (daniele.melini@ingv.it). Please note that installing tools like gfortran or any graphic tools to visualise the data is left to the participant - please only contact Giorgio and Daneile with questions about SELEN.

Before arriving to the school participants should:

1) Have the program running - execute the "test run" described in the paper. 2) Read (at least look at) the theory in the main paper.
