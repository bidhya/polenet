Installing Sites in Northern Victoria Land by Eric Kendrick

2014-12-29
I'm back at Zuchelli station after spending about five days at Hallett Camp. We installed the VL01 GPS station the day we arrived there. Weather was nice, just a bit of snow one day, otherwise mostly sunny. It was truly spectacular scenery. I shot lots of video - great place to be flying around in a helicopter!   Mario Zuchelli Station The helicopter at Tombstone Hill, aka VL01
