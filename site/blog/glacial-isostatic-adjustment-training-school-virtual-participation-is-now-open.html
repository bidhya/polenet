Glacial Isostatic Adjustment Training School Virtual Participation is now open

2015-09-11
If you would like to attend our Glacial Isostatic Adjustment Training School lectures virtually, then please follow the link to the Training School home page. http://polenet.org/?page_id=2261 There you will find dates, lecture titles, start times and links to the webinar meeting room. Please be sure to read the helpful hints to ensure you can get the full benefit of the lectures. Attendees will be able to see the lecture slides, hear the lecturer live and also ask questions through a comment window. We look forward to your participation!
